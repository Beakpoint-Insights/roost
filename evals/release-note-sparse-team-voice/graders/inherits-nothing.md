The team's voice file sets only titles, the body and the ending, and it's the team's whole voice. It has no title word limit, so the product team's 10-word title stands, and roost's fallback rules (such as its 2-to-7-word titles in Title Case, or its "New · date" category line) don't apply.

It passes when the note's title is "Email alerts when a project's daily spend passes your threshold" (sentence case, all 10 words), its body is one short paragraph with no bullets, the note ends with "Cheers, the Acme team", and the reply doesn't say it used or mixed in roost's fallback or default voice.

It fails if the title is shortened or reworded, or is in Title Case; if the body has bullets; if the note ends with anything other than "Cheers, the Acme team" (a different sign-off, or a byline after it); or if the reply says it followed roost's fallback voice for any rule.
