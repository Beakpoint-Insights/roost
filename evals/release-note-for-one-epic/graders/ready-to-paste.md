It passes when the reply gives one finished note that can be pasted as it is: a title, a line with its category and date, the body and a closing line, all together with nothing interrupting them. The evidence table and a short list of checks or questions come after the note (or the table may come before it), never in the middle of it.

It fails if the note is split across the reply with commentary between its parts, if it's missing a title or a date, or if the reply gives two alternative versions of the note.
